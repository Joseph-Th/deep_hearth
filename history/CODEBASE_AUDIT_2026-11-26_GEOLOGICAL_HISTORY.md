# Codebase audit: jointly feasible geological measurement history

## Confirmed defects

Trusted load replayed each acquired sample against the authored method, historical body identity, and current/initial mass, but did not compare physical resource-scale evidence across acquisitions. Reversing two individually possible bands could imply regrowth of an extractable body that can only lose mass. Acquisition ticks could also move backwards in persistent allocation order. The old executable loaded both altered histories successfully. A coarser intervening sample could conceal an impossible increase even when the adjacent bands overlapped.

## Correction

The knowledge owner requires nondecreasing observation ticks in persistent ID order. Cross-owner evidence replay retains the largest feasible positive mass per uniquely localized deposit, using the same canonical quantizer as live acquisition and the established current and initial mass bounds. Later samples cannot exceed that feasible bound. The limiting observation identity is retained for precise diagnostics even when intervening samples are coarser. A wider interval does not erase tighter physical evidence. The check is deterministic, local to validation, and stores no additional persisted state.

The former boolean historical bucket matcher is replaced with an exact maximum-feasible-mass calculation. Ordinary bucket upper endpoints are exclusive; representational-ceiling buckets are checked through the canonical quantizer rather than treating their widened lower endpoint as exact. Existing authored-method checks and diagnostics remain in place. No sample is rewritten and no new live reserve information is exposed. The technical design documents this invariant.

## Verification

Existing ceiling cases now assert the exact feasible maximum; additional arithmetic coverage checks exclusive upper endpoints, clipping to an earlier bound, and rejection of zero physical mass.

## Scope and limits

Reviewed fieldwork acquisition, tick ordering, geological generation/depletion lifecycle, the knowledge owner, historical evidence validation, and mixed-resolution survey authoring. The new constraint proves a feasible nonincreasing sequence, not an exact extraction ledger or authentic save provenance. Existing same-tick geological availability semantics remain unchanged; generated-world survey admission already requires authority over its whole footprint, and no ordinary same-tick generation conflict was reproduced. No benchmark or performance gain is claimed. All 12 pre-existing terrain/custody modifications remain preserved and excluded from the commit. Verification ran on the combined working tree rather than a clean isolated checkout.
